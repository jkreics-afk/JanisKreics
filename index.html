About

The full picture.

Jānis Kreics is an entrepreneur supplying professional services to the construction industry, operating two companies based in Latvia that specialise in architecture, digital construction, and building design. His career sits at the intersection of sustainable building practice and digital transformation, where technical rigour and business strategy meet.

As a LEED AP practitioner, he brings environmentally responsible thinking to every project. He currently serves as BIM Manager on one of Europe's most significant infrastructure programmes, reshaping how the Baltic states connect to the wider European rail network.

His work spans Project Management, Project Controls, Building Information Modelling, and the integration of digital tools into construction workflows, helping teams work smarter, deliver on time and within budget, and reduce environmental impact. An Engineering degree combined with an MBA gives him fluency across both technical and executive registers that few practitioners bring to the table.

Through a competitive international internship programme, he trained with one of the world's leading construction companies. That experience shaped his professional trajectory and continues to inform how he approaches every project today.
